Clear Media — Operator

Built from nothing, over ten years, into one of Colombo's largest production companies.

Clear Media is where the operator side of the work lives — the company Karvin started, built, and still runs day to day.

Origin

Ten years, one client at a time.

Clear Media didn't start with investors or a studio. It started the way most production companies in this market do — with a camera, a small team, and a willingness to take on the jobs bigger companies didn't want yet. A decade later, it's grown into one of the largest video production companies in Colombo, without losing the part that made it work in the first place.

The story here isn't a straight line. It's a decade of learning production, then learning business, then learning how to do both at scale — the same "still figuring it out" approach that runs through everything else on this site.
