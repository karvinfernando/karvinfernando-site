Investments — Beyond the Frame

Building the infrastructure most people in this industry never attempt.

Most people who work in production stop at the work itself. I've bet heavily on the physical and business infrastructure underneath it — starting with Colombo Production Studio.

Flagship Investment

Colombo Production Studio

A significant, heavy investment into what stands as one of the largest production studio facilities in Sri Lanka. This isn't a rented soundstage for one project — it's permanent infrastructure, built to support production at a scale the local industry rarely sees.

The facility houses multiple venture spaces under one roof, including a dedicated podcast studio and a portrait studio — built so that production, content, and post-production infrastructure exist in one place rather than being pieced together project by project.
