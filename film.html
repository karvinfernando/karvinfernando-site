Film — Directing & Cinematography

Sri Lankan stories, told from behind the camera and in front of it.

Karvin Fernando has spent over a decade as a director of photography, working across commercial and narrative production in Sri Lanka — and the last several years pushing further into directing his own work. This page isn't a highlight reel. There isn't one yet. What's here is the actual state of the work: a feature in development, the stills behind it, and the infrastructure he's built to make bigger things possible.
